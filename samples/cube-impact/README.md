# Cube impact showcase

`cube-target-80mm.stl` is a closed 80 mm cube made from 12 triangles. Import it as
millimetres, keep **Center at origin** enabled, assign S355JR to the target, and use
a 50 mm 42CrMo4 spherical impactor at 30 m/s along negative Z.

The example is intentionally small and reproducible. Its outputs are a numerical
demonstration of the current explicit-lattice backend, not certified design data.
